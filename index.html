Running

When I'm not at the keyboard, I'm chasing finish lines.

Finish lines are not only the in the running event, but it mean achieving my own goal. For example my finish lines:

  • Run 5 times and 10 KM each per week for 3 months
  • Weekly increasing 10 km until I reach 100 km per week
  • Collecting each category 5k, 10k, 21k, 42k, and 50k
  • Run 21km in under 2 hours
  • Run 10km in under 50 minutes

I started running without any specific purpose, not to get healthier or lose weight, just to run because of stress. My journey truly began when I completed my first 10km. It wasn’t fast, and it wasn’t easy, my legs hurt, but I wasn’t much tired. At that moment, I surprised that how far I run, and I loved the feeling. It made me want to run again and again.

However, after a month, my motivation began to fade. That’s when I understood that to maintain this habit, I needed discipline. But discipline alone wasn’t enough to make running enjoyable, so I approached it creatively by setting my own challenges.

One of my first challenges was to run five 10km sessions per week consistently. I stuck with it for three months and felt a sense of accomplishment. Another challenge I set for myself was to reach 100km in a week without any injuries, a goal that pushed my limits while teaching me the importance of balance and recovery.

Running has become more than just a habit for me, it’s a way to challenge myself, grow, and stay connected to something I truly enjoy.

The journey has started in December 2023 and keep going.

My First Ever Accumulated 100km in 15 days

2023:

I started to run about 2km that finished around 12min. I was able to continue around 1 month by rest many days in between. My goal at that time is about going out for exercise. So, from a person who never exercise before I believed that just run fast otherwise no way to make sweat. However, after each run, it is always tiring and there was no motivation to continue again - I only remembered about the tiring moments. As the result, I quit then restart again many times. But I never know how to make it works. It maybe the body did not adapt with this change yet. Additionally, I did not see any different with this short run exercise (15mins exercise), that bring to be not motivated for continue.

A Domino Effect (15 December 2023):

This was a triggered point. I started again without any goal and did not force myself for high speed and long distance run. The only thing in my mind was running slowly as far as I could go. Surprisingly, after passing 2-3 km, I felt normal. Thus, I just kept go on. After reaching around 5km, I am happy, and though about trying 10km. Even, it hurt my legs after 8km. I was also tired but less than previous ran. Thus, the last 2km left was not the big deal to reach my first 10km. Therefore, I was hopeful of finishing 10km. As the result I finished around ~8min/km. It is a very good start and motivated me to continue with slowly run. First week, it hurt my legs and there are various symptoms occur, but it became better and then almost be normal in the upcoming week. Until now I am able to finish my 5km many time in streak. It is surprise that when I look in summary of last over 10 days, I was able to run roughly 60km. There was still almost 1 week before end of the year. It is a domino effect. I would love to reach 100km. The last 5 days, I ran with 5km (2 times), 6km (3 times) and 12km (1 time). That is why my first 100km happened.


Base on my observation, leg & heart rate are two important things to maintain and train. Running fast in short distance, the heart rate will be high but leg maybe not hurt. Running slowly in long distance, the heart rate would be normal while legs would be hurt most. So, train the leg to be stronger we would be reach more long distance.
