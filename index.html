My Hobby – Running

When I'm not at the keyboard, I'm chasing finish lines.

The finish line is not just the one in the running events, but it is every goal which I set for myself in daily running, and I have achieved it. Also, I have some awards to celebrate. For example, I will not buy my first ever carbon-plat running shoe until I can run 10km under 50 minutes. It became a challenge and inspiration at the same time. These are some of my finish lines:

  • Run 5 times and 10 km each per week for 3 months
  • Run increasingly 10 km every week until I reach 100 km per week
  • Collecting every category race in running events 5k, 10k, 21k, 42k, and 50k
  • Run 21km under 2 hours
  • Run 10km under 50 minutes

From my experience, there is a cycle of running. At the beginning, it started with motivation. Motivation can stay up to months and we do not need to push myself at all. It makes us feel good and want to do it again and again. We only need take care of the body, because we maybe want to run without even rest day. Next, when the motivation is starting disappeared. We start pushing ourselves to run. This is a crossroads, where we can easily stop running. When there is not more motivation, we need the reason to support. At that point, I decided to keep continue, because it was a good habit for my health. However, it is not simple as it is. The most difficulty is pushing ourself every time. To deal with it, I create own challenges and focusing achieve it.

One of my first challenges was to run five 10 km sessions per week consistently. I stuck with that plan for three months and felt a sense of accomplishment. Another challenge I set for myself was to reach 100km in a week without any injuries and it took up to 6 weeks to complete. Each challenge will push the limit which need to find a ways and stick to the plan to achieve it, which is called discipline. The more I can get over my laziness to follow the plan, the more energy I gain. It make me understand why people say: running is not about winning against others, but it is about winning against ourself. We do not need to seek outside motivation, but winning ourself is the greatest motivation. The finish line rewards me with the new motivation, energy, and lesson which keep me continue with running.

Everything always has day one, my day one was in 2023. Below is my story about my first 15 days from day one.

My First Ever Accumulated 100km in 15 days

Before the Real Journey Began, 2023

I started to run about 2km that finished around 12min. I was able to continue around 1 month by rest many days in between. My goal at that time is about going out for exercise. So, from a person who never exercise before I believed that just run fast otherwise no way to make sweat. However, after each run, it is always tiring and there was no motivation to continue again - I only remembered about the tiring moments. As the result, I quit then restart again many times. But I never know how to make it works. It maybe the body did not adapt with this change yet. Additionally, I did not see any different with this short run exercise (15mins exercise), that bring to be not motivated for continue.

A Domino Effect, 15 December 2023

This was the date when the journey began. I started again without any goal and did not force myself for high speed and long distance run. The only thing in my mind was running slowly as far as I could go. Surprisingly, after passing 2-3 km, I felt normal. Thus, I just kept go on. After reaching around 5km, I am happy, and though about trying 10km. Even, it hurt my legs after 8km. I was also tired but less than previous ran. Thus, the last 2km left was not the big deal to reach my first 10km. Therefore, I was hopeful of finishing 10km. As the result I finished around ~8min/km. It is a very good start and motivated me to continue with slowly run. First week, it hurt my legs and there are various symptoms occur, but it became better and then almost be normal in the upcoming week. Until now I am able to finish my 5km many time in streak. It is surprise that when I look in summary of last over 10 days, I was able to run roughly 60km. There was still almost 1 week before end of the year. It is a domino effect. I would love to reach 100km. The last 5 days, I ran with 5km (2 times), 6km (3 times) and 12km (1 time). That is why my first 100km happened.


Base on my observation, leg and heart rate are two important things to maintain and train. Running fast in short distance, the heart rate will be high but leg maybe not hurt. Running slowly in long distance, the heart rate would be normal while legs would be hurt most. So, train the leg to be stronger we would be reach more long distance.
